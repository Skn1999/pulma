Version 2: the message is now about the City of Espoo's events and employer services, with Pulma as how the event works. Same content in every option, only placement and timing change. Claims link to the city's own pages (fetched 14 Nov 2026); terms and eligibility belong to the city, so the app links out instead of restating amounts. Pick one; nothing in the app changes until you do.

The message (shared by all options)
  1. Meet people you won't find on job boards. Jobseekers from Espoo's employment services and international professionals already living in the region, including experienced career changers.
  2. Free, with the city behind you. The city's recruitment services for employers are free of charge, and Business Espoo's specialists help with recruiting, including from abroad (Talent Boost).
  3. Support with hiring costs. You may be eligible for a pay subsidy or the Espoo supplement for employment, and for EU recruits the Targeted Mobility Scheme. Check the terms.
  4. An hour well spent. With Pulma you bring one real problem, read short answers written for it, and meet only the people you choose at your own table. So far: 4 answers per problem, 90% replied to on time, 8 conversations booked.
